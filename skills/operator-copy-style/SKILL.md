# Operator Copy Style

Use for emails, landing pages, social, proposals, and any text a customer might read.

## Rules

1. No em dashes. Prefer periods, commas, colons, or two short sentences.
2. No emoji in operator/business copy unless the brand explicitly wants them.
3. Lead with the point. Cut throat-clearing openers.
4. Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over buzzwords.
5. Keep paragraphs short. One idea per paragraph when possible.

## Rewrite pattern

Bad:

```text
We help teams — especially founders — unlock leverage with AI.
```

Good:

```text
We help founders cut busywork with practical AI systems.
```

## Checklist before send

- [ ] No em dash characters
- [ ] No "just circling back" / "hope this finds you well"
- [ ] CTA is singular and obvious
- [ ] Claims are specific enough to verify
